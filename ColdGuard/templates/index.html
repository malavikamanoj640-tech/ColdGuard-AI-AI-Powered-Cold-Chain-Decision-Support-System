How It Works

Monitor → Predict → Act

ColdGuard turns shipment data into actionable intelligence through a simple four-step process.

01 — MONITOR

Collect Conditions

Temperature, humidity, expiry, storage and logistics-stage information are captured for every shipment.

02 — PREDICT

Calculate Risk

The machine-learning model analyzes current conditions and estimates future cold-chain violation risk.

03 — EXPLAIN

Understand Why

Risk factors and model outputs are presented in a simple format so operators can understand the reason behind the prediction.

04 — ACT

Prevent Loss

Alerts, interventions and what-if simulations help operators choose corrective actions before cargo is compromised.

About ColdGuard

Predictive Intelligence for Cold-Chain Logistics

ColdGuard AI is designed to help logistics teams move from reactive shipment monitoring to proactive risk management.

Instead of waiting until a shipment arrives damaged, ColdGuard evaluates current shipment conditions, predicts future cold-chain risk and provides actionable information for intervention.

The platform combines machine learning, shipment tracking, operational analytics, alerts and intervention recommendations into one centralized control tower.
